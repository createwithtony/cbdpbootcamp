About this programme

This Programme is bult to help Student learn basic web development and tech skills one step at a time.
The CBDP Basic Tech Skills Program is designed to equip senior secondary school students (SS1–SS3) with practical,
high-impact digital skills before they step into tertiary education or the workforce.
In today’s world, technology isn't just an advantage—it's a fundamental language. Our curriculum bridges the gap between basic computer literacy and practical digital creation.
Through hands-on learning, students go beyond consuming technology to building it, mastering core web development concepts, problem-solving, and foundational tech tools.

Students Learn:

  • Web Development Foundations: Building structured, modern websites using HTML and CSS.
  • Practical Tech Literacy: Navigating modern software tools and digital workspaces.
  • Problem-Solving & Design Thinking: Turning raw ideas into functional, interactive web projects.
